## 2. Executive Summary

- **Six of 24 slides shipped with unfilled `IMAGE: …` placeholder text instead of actual images.** Slides 12, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 all show a gray dashed-border box containing the prompt that was meant for an illustrator. This is the single most damaging issue in the deck — it tells an institutional reader "this is a draft." Fix before any external use.
- **The narrative arc is strong.** Landscape → Market → Shifts → Risks → Frontier → Takeaways is a clean institutional structure, the takeaway slide (22) lands well, and the bubble-test (14) and chokepoint-map (15) are genuinely good. The bones are right.
- **Slide 11 contradicts itself.** The bar chart shows Tech revenue growth 22% / P/E 23×; the footer band on the same slide says "Tech median: ~26× fwd P/E on ~17% forward revenue growth." Two different numbers for the same metric on the same slide. This will be the first thing a sophisticated reader catches.
- **Slide 8 has a layout bug:** the stacked-bar labels for "SOVEREIGNS" and "ENTERPRISE" wrap mid-word ("SOVEREIGN S", "ENTERPRIS E"). Visible at first glance.
- **Date inconsistency throughout.** Cover says "May 2026"; data is "as of Apr 17/22, 2026"; today is Apr 27, 2026. Either the cover is forward-dated for a roadshow, or the underlying data is ten days stale. Pick one and reconcile (slides 1, 10, 11, 13, 14, 22).
- **Three-strike repetition on most cards-style slides.** Slides 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13 each have a title, a subhead, AND a bottom callout band that paraphrase the same sentence. Pick one — the other two are noise.
- **Several titles are still topic labels, not takeaways** (slides 3, 6, 9, 14, 15, 21). The deck's best titles (4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17) prove the author can write full-sentence headlines — these six just got missed.
- **Slide 4 has a math error in the headline.** "Sixty years of AI in six steps" — but the timeline starts in the 1950s and ends in 2025+. That's 70–75 years, not 60.
- **The lab valuations on slide 10 are highly speculative and need a verifiable "as of" anchor.** $852B OpenAI, $380B Anthropic, $1.25T xAI/SpaceX merger — even with the citations, these are large enough to need explicit primary-source links, not just publication names.
